What Is the L-1A New Office Visa?
A Business Visa Built for Companies Going Global

The L-1A is a US intracompany transferee visa for managers and executives. The "new office" L-1A is the version for companies that don't yet have a US presence — it lets you transfer from your Indian company to a newly established US office so you can set it up, hire a team and grow it. For Indian entrepreneurs, it's one of the most practical routes to expand your business to the USA without winning a lottery or making a fixed-dollar investment.

Common myth

  • "I need to invest a large fixed amount, like EB-5."
  • "Only IT or tech companies qualify."
  • "My company must be big with hundreds of employees."
  • "It's an investor visa like the E-2." (Indian citizens aren't eligible for E-2 by treaty.)

The reality

  • No minimum investment is set by law — you need funds that realistically support your US business plan.
  • Every industry is eligible — services, trading, manufacturing, retail, food and more.
  • Small and mid-sized businesses qualify — there's no minimum turnover or headcount.
  • It's a business visa based on your company and your role, not on your investment.

L-1A New Office Requirements — What USCIS Looks For

A new office L-1A petition must show these elements clearly. Most denials come from weak evidence on one of them — which is why we start with a free L-1A business assessment.

Operating Indian companyYour business in India is actively doing business and will keep operating while you're in the US.
Qualifying relationshipThe Indian and US companies are related as parent–subsidiary, branch or affiliate, with clear ownership and control.
1 year of employmentYou've worked for the Indian company for at least 1 continuous year within the last 3 years.
Managerial / executive roleYour role in India — and your planned US role — involves directing the business, managing people or a key function, and making decisions.
Secured US premisesA physical office (a lease or similar) is secured in the US before filing. A home address alone usually isn't enough.
Growth within 1 yearA business plan shows the US office will support a managerial or executive position within one year — staffing, revenue and funding.
Financial capacityProof that funds are available to set up and run the US office. There's no set number; it must match your plan.

L-1A New Office FAQs — Questions Indian Business Owners Ask Us

Is the L-1A an investment visa?

No. The L-1A is an intracompany transferee business visa. US law sets no minimum investment amount. You need enough funds to genuinely set up and run the US office, and the right figure depends on your business plan — a consulting firm needs far less than a manufacturing unit.

Which businesses are eligible?

All industries — IT, manufacturing, textiles, pharma, restaurants, trading, logistics, consulting, jewellery, healthcare, education and more. What matters is a real operating business in India, a qualifying relationship with the US company and a managerial or executive role.

Do you help with US office lease, company registration and bank account opening?

Yes. We handle the full process end to end: finding and leasing US office space, registering your US company, getting your EIN, opening a US business bank account, writing the business plan, filing the L-1A petition, preparing you for the visa interview and setting up payroll and compliance after you arrive.

Can I open a US company and bank account while I am still in India?

Yes. US company registration, EIN and office leasing can be completed remotely from India. We also guide you through opening a US business bank account, with options that suit non-resident owners.

How long does the L-1A New Office process take?

Timelines depend on how quickly the US company and office are set up, USCIS processing and consulate appointment availability. Premium processing gives a USCIS decision within 15 business days of filing. Many clients complete the full journey in a few months.

Can a small business or proprietorship from India qualify?

Yes. There's no minimum turnover or employee count. Small and mid-sized businesses qualify when the Indian company is genuinely operating and has enough structure for you to work in a managerial or executive capacity.

How long is the L-1A New Office visa valid?

A new office petition is approved for up to one year. You can then extend in increments of up to two years, to a maximum of seven years in L-1A status. The first extension requires showing the US office is operating and supports your managerial role.

Do I need to have a US office before applying?

Yes. You must secure physical premises for the US business before the petition is filed. We help you choose a location and lease that fits your plan and satisfies USCIS.

Can my spouse work in the US?

Yes. L-2 spouses are authorized to work incident to their status. Unmarried children under 21 can live and study in the US on L-2.

Can the L-1A lead to a green card?

Yes. Multinational managers and executives may qualify for the EB-1C green card category, which does not require PERM labor certification. We plan your L-1A case with the green card in mind from day one.

How is L-1A different from the H-1B or E-2?

H-1B is employer-sponsored and subject to an annual lottery; L-1A has no cap or lottery. E-2 is a treaty investor visa that Indian citizens aren't eligible for. For Indian business owners, L-1A is often the most direct way to expand into the US.
