Why Settling-In Matters
A Visa Gets You Here. We Help You Belong Here.

When you relocate to the United States on an L-1A, L-1B, or EB-1C, the excitement of approval quickly meets the reality of starting over: no credit history, no social security number, no school for the kids, no idea which neighborhood is right, and a hundred small systems that work differently than back home.

This is where most families struggle — and where most immigration firms stop. USAIS doesn't stop at the visa. Because we operate 16 physical US offices, we provide real, on-the-ground settling-in support during your first year, when you need it most. We've helped business owners from India, the Gulf, Europe, and Africa turn an intimidating move into a smooth, confident arrival.

Think of us as your relocation partner and soft-landing concierge — handling the logistics of life in America so you can concentrate on what you came to do: grow your business and settle your family.

Common Questions
Settling-In Services FAQs
Is settling-in support included with your visa services?
Settling-in services complement our visa and business-setup work. We offer them as part of our end-to-end relocation packages so your family's move is handled alongside your immigration and business needs. We'll tailor a package to what you actually need during your free consultation.
Can you really help open a bank account without US credit history?
Yes. Newcomers can open US bank accounts with the right documents (passport, visa, and often an SSN or ITIN). We guide you to newcomer-friendly banks and help you start building credit through secured cards and other proven methods.
How does my spouse get permission to work?
Spouses of L-1A and L-1B visa holders receive L-2 status and are eligible for an Employment Authorization Document (EAD), which permits work for any US employer. We guide your spouse through the EAD process and offer career/networking support.
Which cities do you provide settling-in services in?
With 16 US offices, we support major metropolitan areas across the country. During your consultation, we'll confirm coverage for your target city and coordinate local, on-the-ground support wherever you're relocating.
How quickly can my children start school?
US public schools generally must enroll resident children promptly, regardless of immigration status. The main requirements are proof of residency (your lease/home), age, and immunization records. We help prepare everything so enrollment is smooth and fast.
Do you help after the first year?
Absolutely. While our intensive support focuses on the critical first year, we remain your long-term partner — assisting with L-1A/L-1B extensions and EB-1C green card planning as your time in the US continues.
