Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026. US Area Immigration Services is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with care and transparency.

1. Who We Are

US Area Immigration Services ("USAIS", "we", "our", "us") is a U.S. Immigration Advisory incorporated in Phoenix, Arizona, USA. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. Our registered office is in Phoenix, AZ, and we operate 16 offices across the United States to serve clients worldwide.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, submit an inquiry, or engage our services, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: Full name, date of birth, nationality, country of birth, passport details
  • Contact information: Email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Immigration information: Visa history, employment records, business details, travel history
  • Financial information: Business financial records where required for case preparation
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited (via cookies and analytics tools)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Assess your eligibility for L-1A, L-1B, EB-1C, and other US immigration pathways
  • Prepare and submit USCIS petitions, business plans, and supporting documentation on your behalf
  • Communicate with you regarding your case status, consultations, and updates
  • Comply with applicable US immigration law, USCIS requirements, and regulatory obligations
  • Improve our website, services, and client experience
  • Send relevant updates, newsletters, or service information (with your consent)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following bases: (a) performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request; (b) compliance with legal obligations; (c) your explicit consent; and (d) our legitimate interests in operating and improving our advisory services, where these do not override your rights.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • USCIS and US government agencies as required to process your immigration case
  • Licensed attorneys where your case requires direct legal representation (with your consent)
  • Trusted service partners (e.g., accountants, payroll providers, HR services) involved in your business setup, under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Technology providers who help us operate our website and case management systems, subject to data processing agreements

6. International Transfers

As an immigration advisory serving clients globally, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect your information during international transfers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, to comply with legal obligations (including USCIS record-keeping requirements), and to resolve any disputes. Immigration case files are typically retained for a minimum of 7 years following case closure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@usareaimmigrationservices.com.

9. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve your experience. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function correctly without them.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a revised "Last updated" date. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
