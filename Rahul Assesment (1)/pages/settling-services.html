Common Questions
Settling-In FAQs
Is settling-in support included with your visa services?
Settling-in services complement our visa and business-setup work. We tailor a package to what you actually need during your free consultation.
Can you really help open a bank account without US credit history?
Yes. Newcomers can open US bank accounts with passport, visa, and often an SSN or ITIN. We guide you to newcomer-friendly banks and help you start building credit through secured cards and other proven methods.
How does my spouse get permission to work?
Spouses of L-1A and L-1B visa holders receive L-2 status and are eligible to work. We guide your spouse through any authorization process and offer career/networking support.
Which cities do you provide settling-in services in?
With 16 US offices, we support major metropolitan areas across the country. We'll confirm coverage for your target city during your consultation.
How quickly can my children start school?
US public schools must enrol resident children promptly. Main requirements: proof of residency, age, and immunisation records. We help prepare everything so enrolment is smooth and fast.
Do you help after the first year?
Absolutely. While intensive support focuses on year one, we remain your long-term partner — assisting with L-1A/L-1B extensions and EB-1C planning.

Hiring US Employees

Having US employees is essential for demonstrating genuine managerial or executive capacity — which you'll need for L-1A extensions and EB-1C. We help you:

  • Post job listings on Indeed, LinkedIn, and Glassdoor
  • Screen candidates — resume review, initial interviews, reference checks
  • Handle onboarding — offer letters, W-4 forms, I-9 verification, employee handbook
  • Set up payroll — direct deposit, tax withholding, benefits administration
  • Maintain compliance — labor law, minimum wage, overtime, anti-discrimination policies

Accounting, Bookkeeping & Tax Compliance

  • Monthly bookkeeping — QuickBooks or Xero, bank reconciliation, expense categorization
  • Payroll processing — biweekly or monthly, federal and state payroll taxes
  • Quarterly tax estimates — calculated and filed on time to avoid penalties
  • Annual federal tax return — Form 1120 (C-Corp) or Form 1065 (partnership)
  • State tax filings — franchise tax, sales tax, state income tax as applicable
  • FBAR and FATCA compliance — foreign bank account reporting requirements

Why Operational Success = Immigration Success

USCIS evaluates your L-1A extension based on whether your US business is actually operating and whether you're genuinely serving in a managerial or executive capacity. Revenue, employees, and a functioning business are not just goals — they're immigration requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many employees do I need for L-1A extension?
No fixed number, but USCIS expects your org chart to demonstrate genuine managerial capacity. Having subordinate managers or supervisors significantly strengthens your case.
Can you handle payroll for just 1–2 employees?
Absolutely. We handle payroll for companies of all sizes using ADP or Gusto.
How soon should I start generating revenue?
USCIS doesn't require immediate revenue, but showing growth within the first year significantly strengthens your extension case. Our business development support helps accelerate this.
