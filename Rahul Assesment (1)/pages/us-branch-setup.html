Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a virtual office address for L-1A?
A virtual office alone is generally insufficient. USCIS requires evidence of secured physical premises where your business genuinely operates. We start with an executive suite or physical space and can graduate to a full commercial lease as you grow.
Do I need to be physically in the US for company registration?
No. We handle everything remotely. You sign documents electronically — we manage all filings from our US offices. You don't need to travel until your visa is approved.
Which US state should I register in?
Delaware is popular for C-Corps due to legal flexibility. But for most L-1A clients, we recommend registering in the state where the office will be physically located — this simplifies compliance and strengthens the USCIS evidence package.
What if USCIS visits my US office?
Our physical US offices are legitimate business premises. If USCIS conducts a site visit, our on-site team is prepared and the office is real — not a mailbox. This is a critical advantage no other advisory can offer.
